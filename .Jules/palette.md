## 2024-05-25 - ARIA Labels for Icon-Only Close Buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only close buttons (like Lucide React `<X />`) in modals frequently lack `aria-label` attributes, rendering them opaque to screen readers.\n**Action:** When adding or auditing modals and close buttons, always verify that `aria-label` is present and hooked into the i18n system (e.g., `t("modal.close")`) for accessible icon-only interactive elements.
