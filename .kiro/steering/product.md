# Product Overview

**Shelf** is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) for inventory management targeting small business owners, e-commerce sellers, and artisans.

## Core Value Proposition

Simple, modern inventory management that works anywhere, anytime—even without internet connection.

**Slogan:** _Every item in its place._

## Key Features

### Free Tier
- **Dashboard:** Business metrics overview (revenue, profit, stock levels)
- **Inventory Management:** Product CRUD with quantity, price, cost, and images
- **Sales Tracking:** Record sales with automatic stock updates
- **Contact Management:** Client and supplier records
- **Purchase Orders:** Create and manage supplier orders with inventory updates
- **Advanced Reports:** Sales analytics with filters, charts, and top products
- **Settings:** Currency, low-stock alerts, data management
- **Multi-language:** English, Spanish, Portuguese
- **Offline-First:** Full functionality without internet, local data storage

### Pro Tier
- **Cloud Sync & Backup:** Firebase-powered cross-device sync
- **AI Assistant:** Natural language business insights via Google Gemini
- **Ad-Free Experience:** Clean interface without ads

## Architecture Philosophy

- **Offline-first:** All data stored locally in `localStorage`
- **Progressive Enhancement:** Pro features layer on top of free tier
- **PWA:** Installable, works like a native app
- **Mobile-friendly:** Responsive design for all devices
